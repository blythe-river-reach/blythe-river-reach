Keep this site afloat 🛟

A free, one-person project — the data is public, the domain and upkeep aren’t. If the tide times saved you a wasted trip to the ramp, you can toss a few bucks in the tip jar. No account needed; the site stays free either way.

✚ Add a place

A beach, sandbar, dock or anywhere on the river that isn’t in the list. It stays private to you until it’s approved.

✚ Depth mark at

A mark is one fixed reference where readings are always taken: the toe of a ramp, a dock piling, the tip of a sandbar. Readings from everyone at this mark build its own depth curve.

With landmarks, people can report “water is at the first joint” instead of measuring. The heights work themselves out from readings; typing them just speeds that up.

✎ Reading at

Tap the photo where the water line is.

The time matters most: the river moves several feet a day, so the reading is matched to the level the sensors say this spot had at that moment.

◎ Landmarks on the photo

Tap where each landmark meets the water line, bottom of the ladder first. Tap a landmark’s chip to move it. With two or more placed, anyone can report a reading by tapping the water line, and the page can draw where the water is on this photo.
